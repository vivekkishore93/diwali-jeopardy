round,category,value,clue,answer,daily_double

final,Mythology,,The final clue,The answer,

round = 1, 2 or final · value is optional (blank = 200/400/… by position) · 6 categories × 5 clues fits the TV best · If any clue in a round has daily_double = yes, those are used; otherwise Daily Doubles are placed randomly (1 in round 1, 2 in round 2).

Editing:

Teams

2–4 teams works best. Renaming keeps scores; players pick their team on their phone.

Game settings

Daily Double counts apply the next time a game starts.
Custom sounds: put files in public/sounds/ named think, daily-double, board-fill, correct, wrong, times-up, fanfare, buzz-in, reveal (.mp3/.ogg/.wav). Custom fonts: public/fonts/clue.woff2, value.woff2, category.woff2. Reload the TV after adding.
